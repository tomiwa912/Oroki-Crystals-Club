Our Distinguished Members

Meet the dedicated professionals and leaders of Oroki Crystals Club, united in service and community.

Executive Leadership

Our dedicated executive team guiding the club's mission and vision

Alhaji Mojeed Akanni

Patron

CEO, Galaxy Hotels. A distinguished pillar of guidance, wisdom, and strategic advisory supporting Oroki Crystals Club's leadership, community outreach, and socio-economic initiatives.

Prince Ìbídàpọ̀ Akínjídé Salami

Patron

Prince Ìbídàpọ̀ Akínjídé Salami is a double royal of Osogbo who holds degrees in Geology from Obafemi Awolowo University. With over 24 years at Chevron as a Development Geologist, he currently serves as Hub Lead for Meren Asset and Screen and Capture Coordinator for Nigeria Mid-Africa JV Offshore Assets. A community leader, he is a two-term Chairman of Stanza 838 SCOBA—where he established welfare and scholarship schemes—a Pillar at All Saints Anglican Cathedral, and Patron of Oroki Crystals Club.

Omo Oba Adeyemo Edward Olusegun

Patron

Omo Oba Adeyemo Edward Olusegun, an indigene of Ogbomosho, was born over six decades ago and educated in Nigeria and the USA. He worked with several organisations, including the renowned Euro Sport, before relocating to the USA in 1997. A proud Yoruba ambassador, he served as President of the Ogbomosho Descendants Union in the USA and belongs to several Yoruba organisations.

Pharmacist Wale Ahmed

Patron

He attended Adenle Primary School, Osogbo, Government College Ibadan, and the University of Ibadan. He is a member of the Royal Pharmaceutical Society of Great Britain, where he currently practices as a pharmacist.

Mrs. Olabisi Adebanjo

Matron

A dedicated businesswoman with experience in merchandising and logistics, passionate about trade, efficient business operations, and creating value through strong customer and business relationships.

Alhaji (Engr.) Muideen Bello

President

From Osogbo, he is an accomplished engineer and entrepreneur with extensive experience at major telecommunications firms including MTN Nigeria. He brings strong leadership skills and strategic vision to his role as President of Oroki Crystals Club. His interests in politics, history, and faith inform his commitment to community development and cultural preservation.

Comrade Adebowale Adekola

Vice President

From Osogbo, he serves as an Auditor at the Office of The Auditor General for Local Governments in Osun State. As pioneer Vice President and co-founder of Oroki Crystals Club, he provides strategic oversight and financial accountability. His professional expertise in auditing ensures transparent governance and fiscal responsibility within the organization.

Pharm. Ropo Ogunwale

Publicity Secretary

From Osogbo, he is a community pharmacist and healthcare entrepreneur with multiple pharmaceutical businesses. He serves as the club's Publicity Secretary, leveraging his expertise in health and community development. His strategic approach enhances the organization's public engagement and outreach initiatives.

Engr. Salami Abiodun Olalekan

Treasurer

From Ibadan, he is a construction engineer and project manager at Lanfar Nigeria Limited. He brings his engineering expertise and project management skills to his role as the club's Treasurer. His professional background ensures meticulous financial oversight and responsible resource management for the organization.

Crystal Taiwo Oyebamiji

Assistant General Secretary

From Otan Ayegbaju, he has professional experience working with the Nigerian Postal Service (NIPOST). His interests include politics, sports, and herbal studies, and he possesses skills in entrepreneurship and trading.

Crystal Sakariyah Adekunle Sikrullahi

Welfare Secretary

From Osogbo, he is an experienced professional in the consumer goods industry with roles at Unilever Nigeria PLC and Pfizer Nutrition Nigeria. His background includes working with Givanas Nigeria Ltd and Cybele Cosmetics Ltd in Lagos. He brings valuable expertise in product development and brand management to the organization.

Crystal Olalekan Komolafe

Social Secretary

From Ijebu Jesa, Oriade LG, he is the CEO of Divine Favour Farms in Osogbo and also leads Komo Builders & Properties. His interests include music, meeting people, community service, and traveling.
